THE FRACTRAN LEGEND

Invented by John Conway in 1972, FRACTRAN is a Turing-complete esoteric language consisting solely of fractions. Start with any integer n. Multiply by the first fraction that yields another integer. Repeat forever — or until you hit a wall.

The legendary 14-fraction program above produces every power of 2 exactly when the exponent is prime. Pure mathematical poetry.
